# about

About me

I'm a Computer Science graduate who builds agentic AI systems and ships them as working automation. I design multi-agent LLM pipelines with deterministic fallbacks and hard guardrails, RAG systems, and full-stack AI products in Python, LangChain/LangGraph and FastAPI/Next.js — and deliver them on UiPath (Maestro, Orchestrator, coded C#).

Most of my recent work is built end-to-end with Claude Code driving the uip CLI, turning plain English into automations that actually run. That means agents constrained to a fixed action set, monotonic guardrails that let a model escalate risk but never soften a hard compliance block, and a human gate wherever the stakes justify one.

I speak at UiPath Community Kolkata on UiPath × Claude, and write about agentic automation on Medium — published in Stackademic and Active Minds Hub.
